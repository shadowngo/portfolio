6 bai tap
1
Thao tac tep va to chuc thu muc
File Management
Phan tich chuyen sau

Trong cac du an ky thuat lon - dac biet la khi lam viec nhom hoac quan ly du lieu thi nghiem - viec dat ten tep va to chuc thu muc theo quy chuan khong don thuan la thoi quen gon gang, ma la yeu to song con cua toan bo workflow.

Hay thu tuong tuong: mot nhom 5 nguoi cung lam bao cao thi nghiem ve tinh chat co hoc cua hop kim, moi nguoi luu file theo kieu bai nop cuoi.docx hay final2_chinh.xlsx. Chi sau 2 tuan, thu muc chung tro thanh mot "me cung so". Nguoc lai, voi quy chuan nhu 2025-05_baocao_cohocvatchat_v3_NTT.docx, moi thanh vien lap tuc nhan dien duoc ngay tao, noi dung, phien ban va nguoi phu trach.

Qua bai tap nay, toi da xay dung cho minh nguyen tac 3C: Co nghia - Cu the - Co the sap xep tu dong. Ten tep tot khong chi phuc vu cho hom nay, ma con la "thong diep gui ve tuong lai" cho chinh minh sau 6 thang.

2
Danh gia thong tin hoc thuat
Information Literacy
Quy trinh kiem chung nguon tin cua toi

Trong thoi dai ma moi giay co hang nghin bai viet duoc dang tai, kha nang phan biet thong tin dang tin cay voi thong tin nhieu la ky nang cot loi cua mot sinh vien khoa hoc. Toi ap dung mo hinh SIFT (Stop - Investigate - Find better coverage - Trace claims) ket hop voi bo cau hoi ca nhan.

Buoc 1 - Dung lai va nhan dien: Toi xac dinh tac gia la ai, co chuyen mon trong linh vuc khong, bai dang tren tap chi co uy tin khong (vi du: IEEE, Elsevier, Nature).

Buoc 2 - Truy vet nguon goc: Toi kiem tra phan References. Mot bai viet hoc thuat thieu trich dan hoac trich dan cac blog ca nhan thuong la dau hieu canh bao.

Buoc 3 - Doi chieu cheo: Toi tim it nhat 2-3 nguon doc lap khac co ket luan tuong tu. Neu mot thong tin chi xuat hien o mot noi duy nhat, toi luon dat dau cham hoi.

Buoc 4 - Kiem tra tinh cap nhat: Trong nganh vat lieu, mot ket qua nghien cuu tu 10 nam truoc co the da bi thay the hoan toan boi phuong phap moi hon.

3
Ky nang viet Prompt hoc tap
Prompt Engineering
So sanh Prompt - Thuc te tu mon Hoa dai cuong
x Prompt ban dau (chua hieu qua)
"Giai thich lien ket hoa hoc cho toi."
v Prompt cai tien (hieu qua cao)
"Toi la sinh vien nam nhat nganh Cong nghe Vat lieu, dang hoc ve lien ket hoa hoc. Hay giai thich su khac biet giua lien ket ion, cong hoa tri va kim loai bang cach so sanh tinh chat co hoc cua vat lieu tao thanh tu moi loai lien ket. Dung vi du cu the (NaCl, diamond, nhom). Cuoi cung cho toi 2 cau hoi tu kiem tra."
Phan tich tai sao Prompt cai tien hieu qua hon

Prompt ban dau qua mo ho - AI khong biet toi la ai, can do sau nao, hay muon ung dung vao linh vuc nao. Ket qua nhan duoc se la mot bai giai thich chung chung, khong khac gi Wikipedia.

Prompt cai tien hieu qua vi no cung cap du 4 yeu to vang: (1) Boi canh - toi la sinh vien nganh gi; (2) Nhiem vu cu the - so sanh, khong chi giai thich; (3) Rang buoc format - dung vi du NaCl, diamond, nhom; (4) Hanh dong mong muon - cau hoi tu kiem tra. AI duoc "neo" vao dung ngu canh, tu do tao ra noi dung truc tiep huu ich cho viec hoc.

4
Hop tac truc tuyen hieu qua
Online Collaboration
He thong quan ly nhom cua toi

Trong bai tap nhom hoc ky dau, toi nhan ra rang su that bai cua nhieu nhom khong den tu viec thieu kien thuc, ma tu thieu he thong phoi hop. Toi de xuat nhom dung ket hop Notion + Zalo theo mo hinh phan tang.

Cau truc Notion: Toi tao mot workspace chung voi 4 khu vuc: (1) Kanban board chia viec theo trang thai To-do / In Progress / Done; (2) Timeline gan deadline cu the cho tung dau viec; (3) Tai nguyen chung luu tat ca link tham khao, tep chia se; (4) Meeting notes ghi lai bien ban moi buoi hop online.

Quy tac nhom: Moi thanh vien phai cap nhat trang thai cong viec it nhat 1 lan/ngay. Zalo chi dung cho thong bao khan va hoi nhanh - moi quyet dinh quan trong duoc ghi vao Notion de co audit trail. Nho he thong nay, nhom toi hoan thanh bai dung han va khong xay ra tinh trang "trung viec" hay "bo sot".

5
AI tao noi dung va ban sac ca nhan
AI + Creativity
Trai nghiem thuc te

Khi duoc giao lam bai thuyet trinh ve "Vat lieu nano trong y sinh hoc", toi quyet dinh thu nghiem quy trinh ket hop AI - con nguoi mot cach co chu dich. Toi dung ChatGPT de tao khung noi dung ban dau va goi y cac goc nhin toi chua nghi toi. Sau do, toi dung Canva AI de phac thao layout slide dau tien.

Dieu toi nhan ra: AI rat gioi tao ra bo xuong - cau truc logic, danh sach y, hinh anh minh hoa. Nhung linh hon cua bai - quan diem ca nhan, vi du lien he voi Viet Nam, cau chuyen dan dat mo dau - chi co the den tu toi. Neu khong co buoc nay, bai thuyet trinh chi la "thong tin dung nhung khong co nguoi".

Toi lap quy tac cho ban than: AI = 30% cong viec tao khung + 70% tu duy va sac thai ca nhan. Ket qua la bai thuyet trinh vua chuyen nghiep ve cau truc, vua mang dam dau an rieng ma giang vien co the nhan ra ngay day la san pham cua Thanh Tam.

6
Su dung AI co trach nhiem
AI Ethics
Bo 3 Nguyen tac Dao duc ca nhan
1

Nguyen tac Minh bach nguon goc: Khi AI ho tro mot phan dang ke trong bai lam, toi se luon ghi chu ro rang phan nao duoc AI ho tro va phan nao la tu duy doc lap cua minh. Toi khong coi viec dung AI la dieu can giau diem - nhung su minh bach chinh la su ton trong voi giang vien va voi chinh minh.

2

Nguyen tac Kiem chung truoc khi tin: Toi khong bao gio nop bai chi dua tren output truc tiep tu AI ma khong kiem tra lai. AI co the tao ra thong tin nghe rat thuyet phuc nhung thuc ra sai. Dac biet trong nganh khoa hoc, mot con so sai hay mot trich dan gia tao co the gay hai nghiem trong cho chat luong hoc thuat.

3

Nguyen tac Khong thay the tu duy: Toi dung AI de mo rong kha nang tu duy, khong de thay the no. Neu toi dung AI de lam bai thay minh suy nghi, toi dang tu cuop di co hoi hoc cua chinh minh - va se la ky su rong kien thuc trong tuong lai. AI la cong cu, khong phai nguoi hoc thay toi.

Tong ket va Hanh trinh phat trien
Cam nhan ve qua trinh thuc hien

Khi bat dau du an Portfolio nay, toi nghi day chi la mot bai tap nop de lay diem. Nhung khi ngoi lai va thuc su suy nghi ve tung muc, toi nhan ra day la lan dau tien trong cuoc doi sinh vien toi duoc nhin lai toan bo hanh trinh hoc tap cua minh nhu mot thuc the thong nhat - khong phai tung bai rieng le, ma la mot buc tranh lon ve nang luc so dang hinh thanh.

Qua trinh thuc hien 6 bai tap thanh phan khong chi la hoan thanh nhiem vu - moi bai la mot tam guong nho, phan chieu lai cach toi dang tu duy, lam viec va dat cau hoi. Toi dac biet bat ngo khi nhan ra rang ky nang so va ky nang tu duy phan bien khong the tach roi nhau - biet dung cong cu ma khong biet suy nghi doc lap chi tao ra "robot thao tac nhanh", chu khong phai ky su thuc thu.

Portfolio nay con day toi mot bai hoc ve su trung thuc tri tue: khong phai luc nao toi cung co cau tra loi hoan hao, va viec thua nhan dieu do roi tim cach cai thien chinh la ban chat cua hoc thuat. Mot sinh vien gioi khong phai la nguoi khong mac loi - ma la nguoi biet nhin nhan loi va he thong hoa cach khong lap lai.
